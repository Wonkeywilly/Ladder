Your ladder above is started on every week in the history. Set points are placed from that week's price (or the rolling 52-week high / 2-year closing high if chosen above) and fill at the first weekly close at or below them. Cash not yet used earns interest. The two comparisons are putting it all in on day one (lump sum) and buying in equal weekly slices over the same period (drip).

Best historic plan for each fund

Plans that mostly sit in cash look safe but never get you into the fund, so only shapes that put at least the chosen share of cash to work on average qualify. For each fund we test 1,000 ladder shapes: buy in 0–80% up front, 2–8 set points, gaps of ½× to 3× the fund's typical monthly move, sized equally or tilted either way, measured from the entry price or the 52-week high. Each one runs over the same start weeks and holding period as the backtest above.
These are the best fits to one historical path, 2019–2026 (SMGB from Dec 2020). That path includes the 2020 crash, the 2022 bear market and the 2025 tariff drop, and treats them as a sample rather than a forecast. Plans that sit near the top under several objectives are more trustworthy than a single winner.
